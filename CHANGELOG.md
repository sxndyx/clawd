# Changelog

Every version of Clawd, newest first. Clawd updates himself, so you're probably on the latest already.

## 0.9 · 2026-10-26

Tucked away, Clawd's tab always shows on the screen you're looking at, even over full-screen apps and after plugging in a display.

## 0.8 · 2026-10-23

A tidier menu: Focus holds timers too, and Settings is down to the essentials, with the rest in Advanced Settings in the menu bar. Back, shuffle and next buttons under Clawd pick his scene, Music is a scene of its own, and his bubble follows his head in every scene. Opens at login by default.

## 0.7.6 · 2026-10-18

No more false finishes: Claude Code in the Claude app's Code tab no longer sets off confetti over and over while it's still working.

## 0.7.5 · 2026-10-17

Clawd stays put while you browse: he only dives for videos playing full screen. And if he's tucked away, his tab always comes back.

## 0.7.4 · 2026-10-16

Settings are sorted into groups: Clawd, Media, Claude and This Mac.

## 0.7.3 · 2026-10-16

He dives out of the way for videos in big windows too, stays under through a pause, and pops back out when you leave full screen, with a much bigger splash. Turn on Ripple the video and the picture itself ripples. A scene you pick as his idle stays on, even in Low Power Mode.

## 0.7.2 · 2026-10-15

The play and skip buttons step aside instead of covering the song's name.

## 0.7.1 · 2026-10-10

The welcome tour now walks you through the real menu, one glowing section at a time.

## 0.7 · 2026-10-09

A welcome tour for new friends, a feedback form for bugs and ideas, the song's name when you hover over the album, and bubbles that bow out gracefully.

## 0.6.2 · 2026-10-09

Timers and focus sessions can have Music as their scene.

## 0.6.1 · 2026-10-07

Speech bubbles spring open, and when he's free, Clawd hauls them open himself. New Auto-size bubble setting.

## 0.6 · 2026-10-07

Music is its own idle. Drag the speech bubble's edges to resize it. He dives into the side of the screen when a video goes full screen, suggests a scene now and then, and the menu never runs off the screen.

## 0.5.1 · 2026-10-07

Some finds are far rarer than others. A softer light for the big moments.

## 0.5 · 2026-10-07

Works with chats in the Claude app, not just Claude Code. The case got a sign, and some finds now come with a number.

## 0.4 · 2026-10-05

Collapsible panel, confetti on/off, clicking Clawd jumps back to the exact terminal tab, your first trophy comes sooner.

## 0.3

Music (DJ, dance, album, lyrics), tuck into the screen edge, Done and "needs you" alerts, screenshot perch, battery and late-night moods, walking around, Hiker and space scenes, multi-select scenes.

## 0.2

Automatic updates.

## 0.1

21 scenes, speech bubble, confetti, progress line, focus timer, usage limits, daily stats.
